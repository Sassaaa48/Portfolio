My Story

About Sasa

Bridging the conceptual gap between human-centric design systems and functional code architecture.

I focus heavily on user research, research-driven design systems, accessibility, and high fidelity interactive interfaces. Having designed real-world professional B2B ecosystems, my approach is centered on mapping logical paths to minimize cognitive friction, while crafting delightful interactions that make products fun to use.

I synthesize user empathy into concrete interface variables, components, and token patterns to cultivate seamless alignment and reliable handoffs with engineering cohorts.

Graduating with a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering from Kathmandu University, I possess a deep analytical grasp of software cycles, computational logic, responsive grid structures, and core layout performance.

This background enables me to write modern clean ES6+ JavaScript, develop component-driven declarative systems, and design interfaces that are technically sound and perfectly optimized.

At the intersection of Human-Centric Design and System Architecture, I resolve complex friction. Whether designing real-time driver/passenger platforms under rigorous field testing, or custom RAG document-review AI platforms, I focus on resolving functional bottlenecks.

I love turning intricate raw performance datasets, data analytics, and workflow metrics into elegant, straightforward, and highly digestible interactive experiences.
