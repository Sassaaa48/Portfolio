About Sasa

Designing digital products that are useful, intuitive, and technically practical.

I'm Sasa Dhungana, a UI/UX and Product Designer with a background in Computer Engineering. I focus on creating digital products that are user-centered, clear, and intuitive.

I enjoy designing cohesive user experiences, from mapping out user flows and wireframes to creating consistent design systems and interactive prototypes in Figma.

Graduating with a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Engineering from Kathmandu University, I understand the technical constraints and implementation details of software development.

This background helps me design interfaces that are not only usable but also responsive, efficient to build, and optimized for final handoff to engineers.

At the intersection of Design and Technology, I focus on identifying user friction and creating straightforward, logical solutions that improve product usability.

I enjoy translating complex information, requirements, and user needs into clean, organized, and elegant interactive interfaces.
